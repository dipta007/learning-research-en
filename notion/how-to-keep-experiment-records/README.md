# How to Keep Experiment Records

> [Original Article](https://pengsida.notion.site/caf34717f4c046c69ee7e14ea953c46f)

An experiment record is generally organised like this. The wording does not need to be very detailed; it is enough that you can understand it yourself, since an experiment record is generally for your own eyes.

1. **The purpose of the experiment**: describe why you are running this experiment, and what you want to get from it.
2. **The setting of the experiment**: what kind of data the experiment was run on, and what was changed in the algorithm.
3. **Record the experimental results**: record both the results where performance is good and the results where it is poor, including visual results and quantitative results.
4. **Analyse the experimental results**: observe whether the results match your expectation. If they do not, you need to [analyse why the experiment does not work](https://pengsida.notion.site/1aee6e718de6472f834d13da8f4ff097) ([translated](../how-to-find-why-an-experiment-fails/README.md)).
5. **Next step**: you are the leader of the project. Keep thinking about how to take the next step, and list the experiments to run next, rather than waiting for instructions.
