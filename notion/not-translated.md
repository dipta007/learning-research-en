# Pages reached but not translated

This repository translates his **research-advice** pages: how to choose a problem, run a project, read papers, write, review, rebut, and so on.

His Notion also holds study notes, where he takes notes on other people's talks, interviews, books and papers. Those are linked here rather than translated. Two reasons: they are notes on material that is mostly not his to republish, and a reader looking for research guidance will not be looking for them.

Nothing is hidden by this choice. Every page is linked, so any of them can be picked up later.
